# Platform Scope and Disclaimer

This platform is an educational analysis tool. Its purpose is to help users learn about markets, indicators, strategies, and risk concepts, and to study historical and current data in a structured way. It exists to build understanding, not to direct anyone's transactions.

**What this platform does.** It presents charts, indicators, strategy explanations, scans, and backtests as educational material. It describes how various methods are constructed, the conditions in which they are commonly studied, and the risks associated with each. It frames observations as context for learning and independent judgement.

**What this platform does not do.** It does not execute orders, and it never places, routes, or manages transactions on any venue. It does not hold, custody, or move funds of any kind. It does not tell anyone to buy or to sell, and it does not issue "buy now" or "sell now" instructions. It does not make price predictions, offer guaranteed outcomes, or claim that any approach will be profitable. Nothing it presents should be interpreted as a recommendation to transact in any specific instrument.

**Nature of the content.** All material is general and educational. It does not take into account any individual's financial situation, objectives, risk tolerance, or circumstances, and it is therefore not personalised advice. Any examples, figures, or historical studies are illustrative. Markets involve substantial risk, and it is possible to lose money; the value of instruments can move in any direction.

Independent verification of any information presented here is encouraged before you rely on it.
